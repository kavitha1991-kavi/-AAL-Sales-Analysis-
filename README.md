AAL Q4 Australia Sales Analysis


This project analyses AAL's Q4 sales data across Australia to understand sales performance by state, identify high- and low-performing regions, and provide data-driven insights to support business decision-making.

The analysis focuses on identifying regional sales trends and understanding where sales performance can be improved.

 Project Objective

The main objectives of this project are to:

Analyse Q4 sales performance across Australian states.
Identify states with the highest revenue.
Identify states with lower sales performance.
Compare sales performance between different regions.
Analyse sales trends and patterns.
Provide recommendations and sales strategies for lower-performing states.
Dataset

The dataset contains sales information for AAL across different Australian regions.

Key information includes:

State
Sales/Revenue
Product or category information
Sales representative information
Date/quarter information

The analysis focuses specifically on Q4 sales performance.

 Data Analysis

The following analysis was performed:

Data cleaning and preparation.
Checked the dataset for missing values and inconsistencies.
Analysed sales performance by state.
Calculated and compared total revenue across states.
Identified the highest-revenue states.
Identified lower-revenue states.
Analysed regional sales patterns.
Developed visualisations to communicate the findings.
 Key Analysis
State-Level Sales Performance

Sales were analysed across Australian states to identify differences in revenue performance.

The analysis helped identify:

States contributing the highest revenue.
States with relatively lower revenue.
Regional differences in sales performance.
Opportunities for improving sales in lower-performing regions.
 Business Insights

The analysis provides insights that can help AAL understand regional sales performance and focus resources where they may have the greatest potential impact.

For lower-performing states, potential strategies include:

Targeted promotional campaigns.
Region-specific marketing activities.
Reviewing product demand and customer preferences.
Strengthening sales activities in underperforming areas.
Monitoring sales KPIs regularly to measure improvement.
